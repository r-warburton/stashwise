How the scores work Pocket layout and value for money

Each storage cell gets the area of the pocket it belongs to. The pocket score is the average across all cells. These layouts all hold four slots:

1 pocket at 2 × 24.00
2 pockets at 2 × 12.00
4 pockets at 1 × 11.00

Formula: sum of (pocket area × pocket area) ÷ total internal slots. Higher is better. This compares pocket layout separately from capacity and stash efficiency. A 2 × 2 and a 1 × 4 pocket both score 4.00; check the pictured layout and storage restrictions for the items you want to fit.

Select Pocket score ↓ as either sorting level to combine it with efficiency or price. Missing or incomplete layouts show a dash and sort last.

Cost / efficiency: selected purchase price ÷ storage efficiency. A 2× rig costing 40,000 ₽ is 20,000 ₽ per 1× efficiency. Lower is better. This uses your chosen price source and can be either sorting level.
